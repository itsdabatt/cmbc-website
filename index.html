👋 YOUR FIRST VISIT

You Don’t Have to Know Everything Before You Come.

👕What do I wear?

Come as you are. You do not need special clothes to worship with us.

🕙When should I arrive?

Sunday School starts at 10:00 AM and Morning Worship at 11:00 AM. A few minutes early is plenty.

📍Where do I go?

Come to 115 Longacre Way in Smithers. If you are unsure where to go when you arrive, just ask — we will gladly help.

🧒What about kids?

Families and children are welcome. BYF meets Wednesday from 6:25–7:45 PM.

📖Do I bring anything?

No. Bring yourself, your family, and a Bible if you have one. If not, you can still come.

❤️Will I be welcome?

Yes. We would be honored to worship with you.
